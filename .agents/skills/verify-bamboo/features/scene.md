# Scene and layout

- Enter on a dry bank within the grove, not in the river or behind a boulder.
- Continuous bamboo trunks meet the ground. Leaves/branches must read as attached foliage, not detached fragments.
- Grove frames the phone view without a solid trunk blocking it.
- Ground reads as soil/moss, not rippling water; water is distinct and has readable surface detail without a transmission render pass at LOW/ULTRA_LOW.
- Inspect stream directly, not only from entry. Tour step 3 is Flowing Reflection.
- Deer stays upright, ashore and articulated through idle/walk/alert, including slow or stalled frames. Crane placement meets the bank.
- Reloads preserve layout. Worker proof covers repeatability/exclusion; pixels cover composition.
- Sweep four quadrants in Grove, Clearing, Stream and Deep Forest.
