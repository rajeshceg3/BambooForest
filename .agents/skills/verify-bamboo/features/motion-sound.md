# Motion and sound

- Sound does not begin without choice. Unmute starts playback and Mute stops it; resume and zone mixing work.
- Capture actual app-output audio where possible. Signal/clip/continuity tests do not establish musical quality. Existing environmental stems do not fulfill the shared ethereal music brief by themselves.
- In reduced motion, inspect whether unsolicited wind, particles, animals, tour and ripples stop as intended. Do not claim a pass from the ripple guard alone.
- Exercise low frame rates: deer interpolation must not extrapolate, camera damping must stay bounded. A unit test of coefficients supplements, but does not replace, pixels.
