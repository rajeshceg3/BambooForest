## Narrow defects and local changes

The prior leaf plane had only two longitudinal segments, making a diamond outline. Its vertex shader tapered that already-tapered blade a second time and divided the0.7m tip by0.6 before a fractional power of sin. The upper tip thus entered a negative fractional-power domain. This is invalid math, not proof that it caused the full-scene stalls. Reversed smoothstep edges in transmission were also undefined, and manual vNormal assignment omitted the instance transform.

Static shaping and normals now bake together in createBambooLeafGeometry:6 longitudinal segments and a central fold,24 triangles per blade instead of4. The shader retains wind/flutter but does not retaper or overwrite instance-correct normals. Transmission compares view-space vectors with ordered smoothstep edges and reduced additive glow. Leaf bases distribute along their actual branch matrix instead of clustering at the end; orientations fan outward/downward, preserving every random draw and all original stalk/branch transforms.

Instance counts remain4994 trunks/8800 branches/35126 leaves in the Grove worker test. Exact old/new stalk and branch arrays compared equal; test-foliage pins the stalk hash, counts and finite leaf matrices/geometry. This does increase leaf triangles sixfold to843024 at that count. It is NOT render-cost neutral, and no physical-device performance acceptance exists.
